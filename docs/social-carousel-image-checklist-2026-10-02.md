## Sourcing and generation record

Heap leaching: [James St. John’s Wikimedia Commons photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cyanide_leaching_of_low_grade_gold_ore_rock_piles_(above_Squaw_Gulch,_Cripple_Creek_Mining_District,_Colorado,_USA)_4.jpg), 3008 × 1574, CC BY 2.0. It depicts actual heap-leach ore piles; it does not document a Bart Mining site or expose the liner itself.

Delivery search: Pexels results showed mine haul trucks rather than equipment-delivery vehicles; the relevant flatbed result was paid stock. Maintenance search: relevant Unsplash results included people or required Unsplash+. Generated illustrations therefore cover these two topics without representing a real fleet, workshop or customer site.

Delivery generation brief: unbranded tractor and lowbed trailer, compact jaw crusher secured with chains, anonymous gravel access road, portrait 4:5, no people or writing.

Maintenance generation brief: unbranded slurry pump, matching impeller, bearings and tools on an anonymous maintenance bench, portrait 4:5, no people or writing.

Original approved photos and crops remain unchanged. Several new slides reuse their post’s equipment photo rather than substitute an unrelated machine. High resolution and portrait framing are preferred; existing lower-resolution assets remain subject to the enlargement checks and legacy rights review.

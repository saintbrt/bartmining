Makita Directory Plan

How the Makita concrete-work and metal-work tools join the bartmining.com equipment directory: two new categories and twelve tool-family pages in English and Kiswahili. Each Makita product name, such as "Combination Hammer LXT", appears once, with all its sizes and kit options listed under it. 257 Makita listings, less 2 older models removed, become 255 models under 55 main products, and every model number still appears for buyers who search by model.

Summary

The equipment pages show main products, named as Makita names them: for example Combination Hammer (corded), Combination Hammer LXT (18V cordless) and Combination Hammer XGT (40V cordless). Every size and kit option sits inside that product as a row in its table: DHR242 with its options DHR242Z (body only), DHR242RFJ and DHR242RTJ (with batteries and case). We write one description per main product, not per model. Makita's spelling variants are tidied (for example "Angel Grinder LXT" and "Angle Grinder, 115 mm" both go under Angle Grinder LXT or Angle Grinder).

Directory structure

Two new categories in /equipment

They sit beside the existing categories, with their own filter buttons, in both /equipment and /equipment-swahili. A brand hub at /equipment/makita links all twelve family pages.

Family pages

Twelve pages, grouped from 25 Makita sub-categories

Small Makita sub-categories are combined so no page is thin. Rotary and combination hammers share one page, because buyers choose them the same way (SDS-PLUS or SDS-MAX, corded or cordless). Concrete Planer moves from Makita's metal-work list to concrete tools, and Makita's two "Portable Cut-off" sub-categories are merged.

Page template

What each family page contains

Uses the existing equipment page layout and editorial standard, so the pages look and read like the rest of the catalogue.

Answer-first summaryWhat the tool does and where it is used on a mine, plant or workshop, in two or three sentences.
How to chooseCorded or cordless, battery platform (CXT 12V, LXT 18V, XGT 40V), size and power, with a short worked example.
Main productsOne block per main product (for example "Combination Hammer LXT"), with a short description and a table listing every size and option: model, kit codes and key spec.
Site use and safetyDust, vibration, guards, PPE and how the tool fits plant construction and maintenance.
MaintenanceBrushes, guards, discs and bits, battery care, with the interval table used on other equipment pages.
FAQs and enquiryThree or four real buyer questions, then what to send for a quotation (model, quantity, site, delivery).

Search

How the directory earns search traffic

  • Titles and URLs carry the brand and the tool: "Makita Rotary Hammers in Tanzania: SDS-PLUS and SDS-MAX models", /equipment/makita-rotary-hammers. Brand-plus-tool searches are the highest-intent queries for a dealer.
  • Every model number is on a page in plain text, so searches such as "GA5040Z" or "HR2611F" can land on the family page.
  • Kiswahili mirrors at /equipment-swahili/makita-… with local terms (for example "mashine ya kuvunja zege", "grinder ya kukata chuma"), checked by a native reviewer.
  • Structured data: ItemList of models on each family page, Product with brand: Makita and the model number for each listed model, and BreadcrumbList. No price or stock claims unless confirmed.
  • Internal links: from relevant mining pages (demolition hammers from plant-construction guides, grinders and cutters from the workshop and maintenance content, rod cutters from foundation work) and from the district supply pages.
  • Measure: add a "Makita" topic to the monthly Search Console review and watch impressions for brand, tool and model queries. Build individual model pages only where model queries appear.
Delivery

Build steps

  1. Confirm the items in To confirm with Makita Tanzania and Allan.
  2. Add the two categories (concrete-tools, metalwork-tools) to the equipment catalogue, filters and category marks, in both languages.
  3. Load the product data (docs/makita/makita-batch-1-models-2026-10-07.csv, column main_product) into a typed data file (src/data/makita/), so the product tables are generated, not typed by hand.
  4. Write 12 family pages, 55 main-product descriptions and the /equipment/makita hub in English, following the editorial standard; Bartholomew checks technical claims.
  5. Write the Kiswahili versions and send them for native review (Codex check plus a human reviewer).
  6. Add images: Makita product photography where the partnership allows it, otherwise our own illustrations; record provenance as for other products.
  7. Add structured data, sitemap entries and internal links; run the editorial, FAQ, Kiswahili-directory and image audits and a production build.
  8. Publish, request indexing, and draft carousel posts for the launch.

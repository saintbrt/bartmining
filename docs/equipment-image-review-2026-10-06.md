# Equipment image review — 6 October 2026

## Scope and findings

All 50 current product images were inspected on labelled contact sheets and measured from their source files. Seventeen sources were narrower than 1,200px. The catalogue used 4:3 cover crops while both product templates forced every subject into a 21:9 cover crop. Several images also showed the wrong duty, a crowded installation or duplicated equipment.

## Changes

- Generate 27 new equipment-class illustrations with the built-in image_gen tool, using the saved prompt set.
- Retain 23 suitable catalogue references, encode them without enlargement, and frame the complete image.
- Select separate optimised WebPs through one 50-product manifest for both languages. Original article-cover sources remain available.
- Use contain framing for cards and responsive 16:9/4:3 product heroes; correct thumbnail sizes and provide clear English/Kiswahili captions and alt text.
- Replace the catalogue overview stock image with the shared wash-plant concept illustration.

These are catalogue references and generic illustrations, not model-specific engineering drawings or documentary images of customer installations. Existing reference-image rights/provenance are inherited from the repository and have not been independently established by this image pass. No new stock/manufacturer downloads are introduced.

Mining Consulting Services Across Africa

Independent mining consulting bridges the gap between exploration data and the investment decisions that fund mine development. Whether you are a junior company seeking to advance an early-stage discovery, an institutional investor conducting technical due diligence, or a major considering an acquisition target, objective, experienced geological advice is the foundation of sound decision-making.

What Independent Mining Consulting Delivers

Independent geological consulting provides clients with unbiased technical assessments. free from the conflicts of interest that can arise when geological advice is provided by a party with a financial stake in the outcome. Bart Mining operates as an independent advisor, bringing field expertise and analytical rigour to every engagement.

Project Generation and Target Selection

For companies entering East or Southern Africa for the first time, or seeking to expand their licence portfolio, project generation services provide systematic identification and ranking of exploration targets. The process integrates: review of national geological survey databases, aeromagnetic and geochemical data compilation, structural interpretation, assessment of analogous deposits, and on-the-ground reconnaissance of the most prospective areas. The deliverable is a prioritised list of licence targets with supporting rationale and estimated exploration costs.

Technical Due Diligence

Technical due diligence (TDD) is required when acquiring an exploration or mining asset. whether as a direct purchase, joint venture, or as part of investment or lending decisions. A thorough TDD review covers:

  • Geological database validation and QA/QC review
  • Independent resource estimate review or re-estimation
  • Assessment of exploration upside and risks
  • Regulatory and tenement status review
  • Environmental liability assessment
  • Operating cost and capital cost benchmarking
  • Management team and technical capability assessment

Bart Mining has conducted TDD assignments for private equity funds, streaming companies, royalty financiers and strategic acquirers across East and Southern Africa.

Resource Estimation and Geological Modelling

JORC- and NI 43-101-compliant Mineral Resource estimates prepared by our Competent Persons (CPs) are accepted by the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX), Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE), Toronto Stock Exchange (TSX-V) and London Stock Exchange Alternative Investment Market (AIM). Our resource estimation work covers gold, copper, cobalt, platinum group metals, coal, base metals and industrial minerals.

Exploration Programme Management

Managing an exploration programme across East Africa, with its complex logistics, community requirements, government liaison and multi-contractor coordination. requires experienced operators on the ground. Bart Mining provides resident project geologists, programme managers and camp supervisors for projects requiring full-time technical oversight.

Assay & Laboratory Equipment for Mineral Exploration

June 20258 min readBart Mining Team

Sample Preparation Equipment

A fully equipped core shed and sample preparation facility for gold exploration in Tanzania requires: jaw crushers (Parker or Essa jaw crushers) for coarse crushing to <6 mm, rotary or riffle splitters for representative sub-sampling, LM2 or LM5 disc mills (Essa, Fritz) for pulverising to 85% passing 75 µm, drying ovens (70°C forced air), and a digital balance with 0.1 g resolution for sub-sample weighing. Compressed air blow-out systems and a dedicated wet-cleaning station are essential to prevent cross-contamination between samples.

Portable XRF Analysers

Portable XRF (pXRF) instruments (Olympus Vanta M series, Bruker S1 Titan, Thermo Niton XL5) are standard field equipment on East African exploration programmes. They provide real-time multi-element screening at the rig or in the core shed, enabling informed decisions on drilling continuation and sample dispatching without waiting for laboratory results. Detection limits for Cu, Zn, Pb, Ni, As and Ti are typically in the range 5–50 ppm — adequate for base-metal pathfinder detection and grade control.

Fire Assay Equipment

Fire assay furnaces (LECO, MTS, Thermolyne) operating at 1,000–1,100°C are used for fusion of the lead-capture flux mixture. Cupellation furnaces complete the process. Most commercial laboratories in Dar es Salaam use automated fusion and AAS or ICP-OES finish lines with LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System) integration for electronic results delivery. Turnaround is typically 5–8 business days from sample receipt.

Core Photography and Digital Documentation

Digital core photography — using a Corebox Pro, Core Scanner or custom-built light box — produces standardised, calibrated images of core trays for permanent geological records. Combined with photogrammetric scanning (NextScan, Corescan HCI) for high-resolution mineralogical analysis, digital documentation reduces the risk of data loss and enables remote expert review of core from anywhere in the world.

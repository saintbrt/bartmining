Mining Vehicles & Field Transport for African Exploration Camps

June 20258 min readBart Mining Team

4WD Vehicles for Exploration Camps

The Toyota Land Cruiser 79 Series (single and double cab) is the dominant exploration vehicle across East Africa — a choice driven by spare parts availability at every major town, simplicity of field repair, load capacity and proven off-road capability on Tanzania and Zambia's corrugated laterite roads. The GX variant with factory diff-lock is preferred; suspension lifts and external roof racks for core tray transport are common modifications. Nissan Patrol GU and Ford Ranger 4WD are alternatives with good regional support.

Camp Equipment and Power Supply

Remote exploration camps in East Africa require: canvas or fibreglass-panel prefab camp modules (Weatherhaven, Container Solutions), diesel generator sets (Perkins, Cummins 30–100 kVA), solar-diesel hybrid charging systems for core shed and office power, satellite internet (Starlink has transformed connectivity for remote camps since 2023), and water treatment (reverse osmosis or UV purification of borehole or surface water sources).

Helicopter Support for Fly-Camp Operations

For remote prospects in inaccessible terrain — the Rift escarpments, Selous buffer zone, high-altitude areas of the Ruwenzori — helicopter support enables fly-camp operations with small geologist-led teams. Rotary-wing platforms (Robinson R44, Bell 206, AS350 B3e) are used for personnel transport; larger platforms (Bell 412, AS350 B3e in highland) for core barrel re-supply. All helicopter operations in Tanzania require CAA Tanzania-cleared operators and compliance with ICAO safety standards.

Fuel and Logistics Management

Fuel supply chains for remote exploration camps are a significant logistical and cost challenge. Diesel prices in Tanzania range from $0.90–1.20/L depending on proximity to supply, with significant premiums in remote areas. Fuel management systems (electronic dispensers with per-vehicle tracking) help minimise pilferage — a persistent cost-overrun driver on African exploration programmes. Consolidated fuel delivery contracts negotiated at the country level provide better pricing and security of supply.

Drilling Equipment for Mineral Exploration in Africa

June 20258 min readBart Mining Team

Diamond Core Rigs

Diamond core drilling delivers continuous rock samples for geological logging and JORC-compliant resource estimation. The most widely deployed surface core rigs in East Africa are in the 500–2,000 m depth class. Popular platforms include the Epiroc CS series, Boart Longyear LF90 and LF130, and the Chinese-manufactured ZDY series which dominates cost-sensitive programmes. Key specifications to evaluate: wire-line capability (saves significant rod-trip time on deep holes), pump capacity (minimum 150 L/min for >500 m holes in Tanzania's weathered profiles), and dual-speed rotation for crown-life optimisation in hard, abrasive BIF rock.

Reverse Circulation Rigs

RC drilling provides fast, low-cost sample coverage ideal for oxide ore definition and early-stage exploration. Track-mounted multipurpose units (Sandvik DR560, Rockmore RC series) capable of switching between RC and diamond core are the preferred choice for East African exploration contractors, as they allow a single rig to handle both exploration and resource definition phases. Compressor capacity is critical — a minimum of 900 cfm at 350 psi is required for efficient sample return at 150 m depth; this should increase to 1,200+ cfm for deeper programmes.

Downhole Tooling and Accessories

Core orientation tools (Reflex EZI-SHOT, IMDEX eSurvey) allow accurate measurement of core orientation in oriented holes for structural geology. Wedge assemblies enable deflection of daughter holes from parent holes to test strike and depth extensions without additional collars. Casing shoe assemblies and casing hammers are required for collaring through weak, collapsing alluvial or saprolitic overburden before reaching competent rock.

Drill Site Setup and Environmental Compliance

Tanzania's NEMC and equivalent bodies in other East African countries require drill site environmental management plans that address: containment of drill fluids (bunded sumps), core shed siting away from drainage features, fuel storage protocols, and site rehabilitation on completion.

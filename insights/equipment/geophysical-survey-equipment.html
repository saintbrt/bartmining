Geophysical Survey Equipment for Mineral Exploration in Africa

June 20258 min readBart Mining Team

Ground Magnetometers

Caesium-vapour magnetometers (GEM Systems GSM-19T, Scintrex CsMP) are the standard for ground magnetic surveys in East Africa due to their sensitivity (±0.01 nT resolution) and compatibility with real-time GPS recording. For high-density surveying over small prospect areas, fluxgate gradiometers (Bartington, GEM G-856) measure the magnetic gradient and are particularly useful for structural lineament detection and near-surface intrusion mapping. UAV-mounted magnetometers (GEM GSMP-35U) now enable sub-25 m line-spacing at costs comparable to ground traversing for areas up to several km².

IP / Resistivity Systems

Induced Polarisation surveys in East Africa are dominated by IRIS Instruments Syscal Pro (single-channel multi-electrode), Zonge GDP-32 II (multi-channel gradient array) and AGI SuperSting R8 for 2D/3D profiling. Cable-free systems (Wireless Syscal Pro) are gaining traction in difficult terrain where deploying hundreds of metres of electrode cable is impractical. For deep IP targets (>300 m), larger current transmitters (5–10 kW) and long dipole lengths are required.

Gravity Meters

Scintrex CG-6 and Lacoste-Romberg G/D meters are the instruments of choice for Bouguer gravity surveys. GPS elevation control to ±1 cm vertical is required for meaningful terrain correction in mountainous terrain (Kilimanjaro, Kenya Highlands, Rwenzori). Gravity surveys are most powerful when combined with aeromagnetics — the two datasets provide complementary density and susceptibility information.

Time-Domain EM (TDEM)

Ground TDEM systems (Geonics PROTEM, Zonge TDPE) are used for massive sulphide and graphitic conductor detection. Multi-turn transmitter loops of 50–200 m side length provide investigation depths of 200–500 m. Processing and inversion in software (Maxwell, IXseg2Segy, UBC TEM) delivers conductance-depth sections that can be directly compared with drill results.

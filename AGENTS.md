# Bart Mining working instructions

For public writing, editing, translating or article-layout changes, read and follow [the editorial standard](docs/editorial-standard.md).

Each guide must establish the reader's question, explain the answer with connected prose, provide a practical example or outcome, and finish with a substantive conclusion and relevant next step. English and Kiswahili counterparts must offer the same material guidance and share one article layout.

Private project data under `projects/plant-planner/data/` is intentionally gitignored. Public content may use expressly authorised client-facing scope and prices; never expose supplier costs, commission, margin, reserves, staff pay, private attachments or client details. Keep public examples in a separate reviewed snapshot.

Preserve unrelated user changes. Do not deploy or publish changes unless requested.

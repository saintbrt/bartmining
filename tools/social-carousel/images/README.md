# Social images

Drop sourced or generated images here (jpg, jpeg, png, webp). They appear in
the tool's image dropdown automatically, next to the site's equipment stock.

Rules (docs/BARTMINING-SOCIAL.md §2.5–2.6):
- Stock or generated only. No real photos of our people, sites, yard or kit.
- Generated images: equipment only, no people, no real sites or brand logos.
- Aim for at least 1080 × 1350 (portrait) so full-bleed slides stay sharp.
  The tool flags anything it has to upscale.
- For the October news and informative posts, use category, topic and slide
  number: `news-gold-1.png`, `explainer-ore-2.png`, `product-wetmill-1.webp`.
  Number 1 is the cover; later numbers follow the content slides in order.
  Keep the source format. Exported slides always use `.png` and the same stem.

[`file-names.json`](file-names.json) lists all 80 source files for IG-19–IG-40,
their post and slide, previous names, exported names and byte checksums. Shared
content images have a separate editable copy for each post. The closing slide
continues to use the shared CTA image; its download takes the next number.

Source records for new public-library images are in `sources.json`. Keep the
author, license, source and crop credits in the corresponding post captions.
CC BY-SA photo slides are shared under the source license, as stated in those
captions. The delivery map’s editable SVG is in `sources/`.

The source register does not establish rights for older website images.
See `docs/social-carousel-audit-2026-10-02.md` for those pending checks.

The October informative batch includes six reviewed catalogue illustrations
copied from `public/equipment/website/` into the numbered social files.
The website originals and files still used by earlier posts retain their names.
Their production records are linked in
[`catalogue-provenance.json`](../../../docs/social-informative-image-review-2026-10-07/catalogue-provenance.json).
The new image prompts, diagrams, assignments and rendered review sheets are in
[`docs/social-informative-image-review-2026-10-07/`](../../../docs/social-informative-image-review-2026-10-07/).
